# Taste review

Take a screenshot (or open the page) and answer each question. Fix every "no" before you call the screen done.

## 10-second test
- Can a person with no finance knowledge say what this screen tells them, in 10 seconds?
- Is there exactly one thing that draws the eye first (the hero or the headline)?

## Look
- One accent color only. Red only for broken rules and the lender's limit. Green only for survival and working fixes.
- Enough white space. Nothing touches an edge. Alignment on a clear grid.
- At most 3 text sizes on the screen. Labels muted, numbers strong.
- No borders where spacing would do.
- Nothing decorative (no gradients, no shadows beyond shadcn defaults, no emoji).

## Words
- Plain words (design-spec §6). No "covenant", "liquidity", "DSO" outside the proof drawer.
- Headlines are full answers: "You fall below the lender's limit in week 5", not "Forecast".
- Every number has a unit and a date.
- Buttons are verbs: "Apply fixes", "Approve", "Show proof".

## Trust
- Can you reach the proof for every number in one click?
- Does anything look like it will act without a person? If yes, add a confirm step.

## Motion
- Does the motion explain something (fan-out, lines bending up)? If it is just decoration, remove it.

Write the 3 biggest fixes as a short list, then fix them.
