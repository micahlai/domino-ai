# Domino overview

**One line:** Domino stops cash problems before they start a chain reaction.

**Pitch (20 seconds, plain words):**
Cash is the lifeline of a business. When a customer pays late, a chain of problems follows:
cash drops below what the lender requires, hiring stops, bills go unpaid. Domino plugs into a
software company's tools, learns how its money moves, and simulates every future it might face.
It shows which futures break the company, why, and which fixes make it survive more of them.
A person approves every move.

**Seat it fills:** the FP&A analyst who owns the cash forecast, working with the controller and CFO.

**The wow:** the multiverse timeline. The past on the left. Today in the middle. Every possible
future fans out on the right. "Kestrel survives 7 of 11 futures. With two fixes, 9 of 11."

**What makes it different**
- Learns a new company by itself (CREAO agents read each tool's docs and build the connectors).
- Forecasts cash the way the lender defines it (finds restricted cash the books count as usable).
- Simulates many futures at once, not one forecast.
- Every number has proof. Every fix is simulated before anyone approves it.

**Vertical:** B2B software (SaaS). **Demo company:** Kestrel Data (simulated, based on public filings).
