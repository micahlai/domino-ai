# Demo script (3 minutes)

The demo video opens with step 3. Rehearse with a timer. Keep a recorded backup.

| # | Time | What we show | What we say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00 to 0:25 | Connect screen: sources found, learned, tied to the bank | "Domino starts knowing nothing. It finds Kestrel's tools, reads their docs, and learns them. Every number ties back to the bank." |
| 2 | 0:25 to 0:50 | Multiverse loads: past draws, futures fan out | "This is Kestrel's past. This is today. Each line on the right is a possible future." |
| 3 | 0:50 to 1:20 | Score "Survives 7 of 11 futures". Hover a red line. | "In 4 futures, Kestrel breaks its lender's limit. Here is why: Acme has paid late twice, and $600K of the cash is locked as lease collateral, which the loan does not count." |
| 4 | 1:20 to 1:50 | Click the Acme future: domino chain, proof drawer | "Acme pays late, cash drops, the lender's limit breaks in week 5, hiring pauses. Every step links to its proof." |
| 5 | 1:50 to 2:20 | Toggle "Apply fixes": lines bend up and turn green, score 64% to 93% | "Domino suggests two fixes and tests them in every future. Kestrel now survives 9 of 11." |
| 6 | 2:20 to 2:45 | Judge clicks "Add event" (their own scenario) | "Try your own: what if the bank lowers the credit line?" |
| 7 | 2:45 to 3:00 | Learning screen: error falling, learned rules | "And it gets better every week. It learned Acme pays 3 weeks late and cut its forecast error in half." |

**If something breaks:** press `R` on the main screen to reload cached results. Switch to the backup video if the app is down.
