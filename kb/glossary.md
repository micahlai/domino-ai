# Glossary (plain English)

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| FP&A | Financial planning and analysis: the team that forecasts and plans the company's money |
| 13-week cash forecast | Cash in and out, week by week, for the next 13 weeks |
| Usable cash | Cash the company can actually spend. Excludes restricted cash. |
| Restricted cash | Cash the company holds but cannot use, for example lease collateral |
| Lender's limit (covenant) | A rule in a loan agreement, for example "keep at least $3M of usable cash" |
| Runway | How many months the company can run before cash hits zero at the current burn |
| Burn | How much more cash goes out than comes in each month |
| ARR | Annual recurring revenue: yearly value of all active subscriptions |
| Net 30 | The customer must pay within 30 days of the invoice |
| Churn | A customer stops paying (does not renew) |
| Tie-out | Checking that two sources agree, for example Stripe payouts and bank deposits |
| Low point | The lowest usable cash in the forecast |
| Universe / future | One possible version of the next 13 weeks |
| Resilience | Share of possible futures where the company keeps all its rules |
| Breaking point | The smallest shock that makes the company break a rule |
