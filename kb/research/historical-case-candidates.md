Recommendation: provisionally 2U, subject to a bounded evidence and intervention audit. WeWork is the recognizable alternative; Appgate fits software and lender-limit modeling more directly. No company is selected by the user yet. Amplitude remains calibration only.

This is feasibility research, not a validated financial dataset or completed forecast. Dates below are publication dates unless identified as financial periods. All cases support quarterly comparisons; none supplies a complete weekly operational ledger. Analysis of model fit and intervention options is our judgment.

## 2U: source chain and traps

- The September 30, 2023 10-Q was filed November 9, accepted at 16:45:46 in the SEC record. A November 10 cutoff avoids same-day ordering ambiguity. [Filing record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1459417/0001459417-23-000042-index.htm).
- The Q3 balance sheet reports $41.141 million cash/equivalents and $12.710 million restricted cash. Nine-month operating cash outflow was $17.520 million; total cash decline also includes investing and financing. Management then expected resources to cover the next 12 months. These are different metrics and claims, not one cash-burn rate. [Q3 10-Q, balance sheet and liquidity section](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1459417/000145941723000042/twou-20230930.htm).
- Q4 results were public February 12, 2024, before the annual filing. [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1459417/000145941724000006/twou-20240212.htm). Its [earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1459417/000145941724000006/a2uq42023earningsreleaseex.htm) reports combined cash/restricted cash of $73.4 million, $904.7 million debt, a conditional going-concern warning, and January factoring of up to $86.2 million receivables at an 88% purchase rate. Those factoring terms are later evidence, not November inputs.
- The annual 10-K was filed March 6, 2024, accepted 16:25:17. Do not use its additional detail at an earlier cutoff merely because it covers December 2023. [Filing record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1459417/000145941724000011/0001459417-24-000011-index.htm).
- July 25, 2024: issuer announced a prepackaged Chapter 11 transaction, new capital and debt reduction while intending continued operations. This is an eventual outcome, not proof an alternative operating decision would have avoided it. [Issuer announcement](https://2u.com/newsroom/strengthening-2us-financial-position-for-sustained-innovation-and-growth/).

Implication: show the actual Q4 cash increase even if the frozen forecast predicted deterioration. Separate cash forecast error from debt/refinancing warning. The first reveal need not be bankruptcy; later events can be a dated epilogue. Audit financing terms and one costed operating alternative before selecting the case. Do not retune the model until its held-out result looks favorable.

## WeWork: primary-source alternative

- [FY2022 results](https://www.wework.com/newsroom/wework-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2022-results) published February 16, 2023. The annual report was officially filed March 29, accepted March 28 at 19:07. [SEC record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/0001813756-23-000016-index.htm).
- [Q1 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/000181375623000037/wework2023q1pressrelease.htm), May 9: $284 million operating cash outflow, $59 million capex, $343 million free cash outflow. Q1 10-Q filed May 10. [SEC record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/000181375623000040/0001813756-23-000040-index.htm).
- [Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/000181375623000061/wework2023q2pressrelease.htm), August 8: $205 million cash and explicit going-concern doubt; financing commitments are separate from cash. [Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/000181375623000059/we-20230630.htm).
- Q3 statements were furnished November 21 as an 8-K exhibit, after the November 6 Chapter 11 filing. Do not treat them as a prepetition 10-Q. [Filing record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/000181375623000067/0001813756-23-000067-index.html), [statements/subsequent events](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1813756/000181375623000067/wework2023q310q.htm).

Model fit judgment: quarterly cash bridge and bounded capex reduction are feasible. Lease reductions require fees, delays, consent and revenue effects. Distinguish restricted/subsidiary cash and undrawn commitments. Improving EBITDA does not establish cash sustainability.

## Appgate: software alternative

- Q2 10-Q filed August 31, 2023. [SEC record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1353538/000162828023031140/0001628280-23-031140-index.htm). [Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1353538/000162828023031140/apgt-20230630.htm): 83% subscription revenue, $4.873 million cash, $624,000 restricted cash and $11.185 million operating cash outflow for six months.
- Q3 report filed November 14. [SEC record](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1353538/000162828023038924/0001628280-23-038924-index.htm). [Report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1353538/000162828023038924/apgt-20230930.htm): $3.584 million cash, $5 million quarter-end liquidity requirement and waiver of noncompliance.
- May 6, 2024: issuer announced Chapter 11 recapitalization. [Issuer release](https://www.appgate.com/news-press/appgate-announces-growth-plan).

Model fit judgment: expense changes and lender-limit scenarios are closer to the existing concepts. Existing warnings/waivers belong in the frozen inputs; do not claim the tool discovered undisclosed distress. A waiver is not interchangeable with an unqualified covenant breach or automatic default.

## Selection gate

H1 must verify exact metric definitions, cash reconciliation, units, publication timestamps/timezones and any amendments, then choose one feasible intervention with costs and constraints. All candidates require distinct financial-period and knowledge-cutoff dates. Use the same predeclared evaluation method regardless of which story looks most dramatic.
