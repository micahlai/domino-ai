## E3b: receipts and rule evaluation

- Consume explicit Deal.invoiceId. A linked deal never adds another receipt, including when its invoice is paid. A signed won deal with explicit null adds cash once. A signed won deal with missing linkage fails with an actionable unknown-mapping error; do not guess by customer or amount. Unsigned deals still add no cash.
- Replace E3a's raw-fixture ambiguity expectation with the linked C1 case; keep a separate deliberately unknown legacy case. Preserve 4a9be26 exact rounding and asOf behavior. Do not change the provisional recurring-spend anchor or savings-as-inflow convention here.
- Implement dominoes with C1's optional context. Without context support weekly min_cash only; reject requested rules needing unavailable inputs. With context evaluate the complete ordered changes exactly once using the same schedule as forecast. Reject unresolved bill/change references instead of reporting success.
- Build a reusable internal evaluator returning rule breaches and evidence for dominoes and future stressTest. min_cash breaks strictly below its limit at weekly close. This is weekly forecast coverage, not proof of continuous compliance with a lender's daily rule.
- pay_by_date targets the original bill's due date plus allowed late days. Evaluate the daily schedule with the allocation convention below; same-day inflows precede outflows. A payment deliberately scheduled after the deadline fails even if later cash is healthy. Funding arriving after the deadline cannot cure the breach. Use usable cash, not restricted cash, for affordability. Paid bills are excluded from future obligations. Already-overdue unpaid obligations appear in week zero. Do not silently treat legacy constraints with missing billId as evaluated.
- Daily affordability is a projected funding test, not a bank payment result. Replay from snapshot usable cash on asOf through the last forecast Sunday. Add each day's inflows, subtract scheduled non-bill outflows (payroll, recurring and one-time cash removals), then fund unpaid bills from a queue ordered by effective scheduled payment date and bill ID. All unpaid bills participate, not only constrained bills. Remove their original outflow entries from this replay so they are never deducted twice. Overdue scheduled items become eligible on asOf + 1 day. Pay the head in full only if sufficient usable cash remains; otherwise retain it and stop processing bills until the next day. No partial payments or leapfrogging. Record each bill's first funded day. This conservative convention reserves committed non-bill expenses first; it does not alter forecast's contractual cash schedule or claim actual bank execution.
- A target fails at the end of its deadline if still unfunded. Past deadlines with an unpaid target fail in week zero. Deadlines after the last forecast Sunday are outside the evaluated horizon: omit their breach and do not claim the obligation was verified payable. Survival means no observed breach within the 13 weeks, not clearance of later obligations. Test recovery before/after the deadline, exact-deadline receipts, two competing bills and both horizon edges. The queue convention is a demo assumption, not a prediction of management's payment priorities.
- Runway uses 13-week net outflow / three months and each week's usable cash; nonpositive net burn gives no breach. Compare exact ratios, including fractional month limits, with the repaired money helpers.
- Emit the first breach per constraint, in chronological order with stable ID tie-breaks. Event and cash-drop markers need actual effective changes and schedule evidence; an overridden delay must not survive as a causal claim. Cash-drop markers require a positive prior usable balance and a drop strictly greater than 5%, compared with integer arithmetic.
- causedBy is optional and references an earlier domino only when supported. Without a counterfactual establishing causation, omit the link. Emit a hiring pause or other consequence only from the breached constraint's supplied consequence evidence. Labels say projected funding shortfall, not that a bill was actually paid late. Map dates to the forecast's Monday buckets, including week-zero overdue events.

## E4: scenarios, fixes and history

- scanRisks uses real snapshot records and model inputs, not answer keys. Late-payment probability uses paid invoices observable by asOf: at least three samples, otherwise the documented 0.1 assumption. p90 is total lateness; delay_invoice.days is additional to the base rounded mean, so use max(0, rounded p90 minus rounded mean), not mean plus p90. Omit no-effect candidates. Label default probabilities as assumptions in evidence notes.
- Non-renewal requires explicit renewal classification and its linked open invoice. Missing kind/link is not evidence; the Northwind expansion is not a renewal. Apply documented 0.25/0.5 assumptions only where supported. Do not invent Orbit usage, churn, customer failure or news signals absent from the inputs.
- Spend spikes compare the latest four complete weeks of card records with the preceding four, as of the snapshot: growth strictly over 20% of a positive earlier total yields a one-time remove_cash for the difference in week zero. Cite the contributing records; do not count the same spending from bank and card sources twice.
- Compute impact through forecast, rank by probability times adverse low-point impact with stable ID ties, return at most five. Do not assert Acme always ranks first unless a controlled test establishes that result.
- Support explicit positive scenarios in stressTest. Do not manufacture an automatic early-deal event or duplicate cash for a deal already in the base forecast. Automatic positive-event discovery is deferred until supported timing evidence exists; this narrows the old prompt's demand for a good event regardless of data.
- scoreFix uses ancestorChanges + parent.changes + fix.changes. Require ancestry for a non-root parent, including explicit [] when appropriate. Output changes remain relative and riskIds carry the parent's known context. Do not silently convert missing riskIds to known empty. Status is proposed, never approved/rejected to encode utility. Keep a non-improving scored branch representable; rankFixes filters it out by low-point gain. Preserve forecast's rejection of delayed critical bills.
- Define and test the engine-local rankFixes helper: compare candidates with their scored branches against the same parent, retain positive low-point gain, prefer removal of every broken rule, then gain, then lower risk, then stable ID. Use E3b's evaluator for rules, not text matching on domino labels.
- stressTest generates base, each risk, pairs of the top four ranked risks, and all adverse risks. Deduplicate canonical sorted risk-ID sets. Reject duplicate risk/fix IDs. Universe IDs stay stable when fixes toggle. The all-adverse set excludes nonpositive-impact events.
- Apply selected fixes in caller order exactly once per applicable universe. Missing targetRiskIds or unknown IDs fail; [] is global; nonempty uses OR matching. Universe.fixIds records only fixes actually applied; report.appliedFixIds records selected fixes. Never infer targeting from labels or customer names.
- Survival uses the shared min_cash/pay_by_date evaluator; runway still contributes dominoes but does not redefine survival. Record the earliest failure deterministically. Base weight is 1; other weights are products of risk chances. Normalize surviving weights by total weights. survivedCount is an integer count, distinct from survivalRate, a weighted index over overlapping scenarios, not a calibrated probability.
- Breaking points are finite sensitivity experiments on the no-fix base. Select the highest-ranked eligible risk containing exactly one delay_invoice change for an open invoice whose unmodified receipt is inside the forecast horizon; tie-break by risk ID. Omit delay sensitivity if none qualifies. Search additional integer days from zero through dateDiff(last forecast Sunday, original expected receipt date) + 1, inclusive. Report total days late including the model's rounded mean. Existing scenario delay is not added a second time. Test two open invoices for the same customer to prove selection and the exact final Sunday/Monday boundary.
- For explicitly linked open renewals with in-horizon receipts, search integer losses from zero to their total forecast receipts, allocating losses in expected-date then invoice-ID order. Reduce those receipts internally before the same-day inflow stage of both the forecast fold and funding replay. Do not represent lost receipts as later one-time outflows, which can falsely fund a bill before subtracting the loss. No public Change variant is needed for this internal sensitivity experiment. Test a same-day renewal receipt and competing bill.
- Omit a breaking point if the no-shock base already fails or no bounded shock breaks it. Binary search only a monotone breach predicate; verify minimality against brute force on small cases. Disclose the bounds and allocation conventions in Result.
- history reconstructs requested Monday buckets backward from asOf cash and bank transactions through asOf only. The final bucket is partial through asOf, not a fictitious future close. pastEvents uses observable dated evidence only; omit unsupported churn/usage and future paidAt records. No network, clock, randomness or input mutation.
