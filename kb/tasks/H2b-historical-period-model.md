## Result
- Done: first phase, synthetic only. `quarterBridge`, `quarterAlternative` and `compareRun` live in `packages/engine/src/period.ts` and are exported from the engine index (they are not on the `Engine` contract). Method `quarterly_cash_bridge@0.1.0`, with one target quarter: the quarter after a month-end origin balance. Assumptions:
  - Each input must be available at the cutoff. It must also be in the case currency and of the correct metric. Parsing goes through `HistoricalCaseSchema`.
  - The OCF/ICF per quarter is comparable YTD divided by the number of elapsed quarters. Both YTD facts must share one start date and end at the origin, and they must cover whole quarters with no more than 12 months.
  - Financing and FX each require an explicit cents-per-quarter assumption range. A missing one throws an error; nothing defaults to 0. Baseline assumptions cannot be hindsight or selected after the reveal.
  - Flows are spread evenly across the days of the target quarter. This is a projection assumption, not evidence. The bridge runs on cash including restricted cash. Restricted cash stays at its origin amount, so usable cash = total - origin restricted. Each date gets both predictions (`*_total`, `*_usable`).
  - The nowcast runs from the stale origin to the cutoff date. The alternative reuses the baseline nowcast unchanged.
  - Point values are range midpoints; ranges are all-low and all-high. These are labelled assumption ranges, not statistical intervals.
  - Intermediates are exact BigInt ratios. Each output is rounded once, with a check that it stays within safe integer cents.
  - The alternative needs a counterfactual or hindsight assumption with `oneTimeCost` and `benefitLagDays`. Its `effectiveFrom` must fall after the cutoff date and on or before the target.
  - The cost is paid in full upfront. The benefit (value range = net cents per quarter, low end = downside) accrues from `effectiveFrom + lag` through the target. `effectCents` gives low/point/high, where the downside is the lowest benefit minus the highest cost. A hindsight label or post-reveal selection makes `postHoc` true.
  - `compareRun` reads the run without changing it. Only facts published after the cutoff and by `revealedThrough` can score it, matched on metric, period and currency. When an amendment exists, the earliest publication wins. Error = observed - predicted, and rule outcomes are reported per metric. The other statuses are `not_yet_published`, `incomparable_scope` (same period, different metric or currency) and `no_observation`. Output is validated with `HistoricalComparisonContextSchema`.
